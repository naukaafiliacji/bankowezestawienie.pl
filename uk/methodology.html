BankRanking methodology

The 0–10 score balances cost, everyday usefulness, access and the real value of benefits. A large bonus alone does not guarantee first place.

Costs — 35%

Monthly fees, waiver rules, cards, ATM costs and common transaction fees.

Everyday banking — 25%

App quality, cards, payments and account usability.

Access & fit — 20%

Branches, cash access, travel and user profile.

Benefits — 20%

Cash bonuses, cashback and interest are discounted for complexity and time limits.

Sources

Every offer links to an official bank source and carries a verification date.
