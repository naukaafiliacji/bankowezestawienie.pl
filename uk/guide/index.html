How to choose a current account in the UK

A switching bonus matters, but fees, overdrafts and day-to-day usability matter too.

Start with recurring cost

A switching bonus is one-off; the account may be held for years.

Read the switch conditions

CASS, Direct Debits, deposits and card payments can all be required.

Separate cashback from a bonus

Ongoing cashback should not be compared as if it were immediate cash.

Check overdraft pricing

A no-monthly-fee account can still be expensive if you regularly use an overdraft.
