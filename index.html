Research model

Cash bonus ≠ cashback ≠ interest.

The site now separates the type of monetary benefit instead of displaying every headline number as if it were an immediate cash reward.

01

Cash bonuses

Switching and new-customer bonuses show their activity, deposit or balance conditions.

02

Cashback

Ongoing spending rewards are marked separately from one-off opening rewards.

03

Interest

Promotional rates show their duration and, where relevant, balance limits.

04

Expiry dates

Time-limited promotions are visibly dated and urgent offers receive a deadline badge.

Affiliate-ready, editorially separate.

Each offer has both an official source URL and a separate affiliate URL field. Today they point to the same official bank page. When you receive a partner link, you replace only the affiliate field — the evidence/source remains unchanged.
