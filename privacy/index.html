Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

BankRanking is currently a static information and comparison website. It does not provide user accounts and does not intentionally run first-party advertising or analytics tracking in this build.

Technical data

Hosting and network providers may process ordinary technical request information such as IP address, browser information, requested URL and timestamps to deliver and secure the website.

Contact

If you contact us by email, we process the information in your message to respond and maintain appropriate business records.

Bank and affiliate links

When you leave BankRanking for a bank, affiliate network or other third-party website, that provider applies its own privacy practices.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights over personal information processed about you. Privacy enquiries can be sent to kontakt@bankowezestawienie.pl.
